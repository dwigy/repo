# Cartoon Orbit — Evaluation and Rework Plan

Scored against `UX-PHILOSOPHY.md`. Each screen gets two marks out of 10 for the state before this rework: **E** (expensive, bespoke) and **A** (art-first). Then the changes, ordered by impact.

## Home — E 5 · A 3
**Problems.** Three navigation systems stacked (top colour tabs, folder sub-nav, bottom bar). Four text panels before the first chip. The only chip is small and at the bottom. Explanatory sentences ("Log in every day to keep your streak. Day 0 streak…"). Michroma used for body-length headings that wrap. Points wallet clips at 390 px.
**Changes.**
1. Rebuild as a poster: a 220 px featured chip (the player's chosen showcase chip, or their best) on a living gradient that shifts with the time of day, one line of copy, one primary action (Rip a pack, or Battle when a pack was already opened today). *Large.*
2. Merge daily bonus, free chip and today's three goals into one "Today" card with three checks and a streak drawn as seven orbs. *Medium.*
3. Remove the top colour-tab row entirely; move the points wallet into the frame header beside the wordmark. Keep the Orbit folder sub-nav. *Small.*
4. Cut the Get Started strip and the Orbit Centers list from Home; the binder's series tabs already do that job. *Small.*
5. Headline copy in Barlow Condensed 30 px italic; Michroma only for the small section tabs. *Small.*

## Binder — E 7 · A 7
**Problems.** Good bones: silhouettes, set meters, tier filters. The character header is text-only; a complete set has no payoff beyond a badge; nothing celebrates the moment of completion.
**Changes.**
1. Set completion ceremony: full-screen poster with the eight editions and the largest type in the app, fired the first time a set completes. *Medium.*
2. Complete sets get a permanent gold ring in the meter and a warm tint on the header. *Small.*
3. Default sort puts completed and nearly complete sets first so the showcase leads. *Small.*

## cToon details — E 6 · A 6
**Problems.** The chip does not react to touch. Provenance is absent, so a Legendary feels no more "yours" than a Common. The artwork line is fine print.
**Changes.**
1. Pokeable chip: drag to tilt with the gloss following the finger, tap to bounce. *Medium.*
2. Flip to the back: mint number, when and how it was acquired. Store provenance on first acquisition. *Medium.*
3. Artist byline as a proper line, not fine print. *Small.*

## cMart and pack ceremony — E 8 · A 8
**Problems.** The rip is already the best moment in the app. The strip follows the finger but nothing peeks out; the summary ends with a plain button; the sound is a beep.
**Changes.**
1. A sliver of the top chip rises out of the pack as the tear progresses. *Small.*
2. Designed sound kit for grab, tear, burst, flip and the tier chimes. *Medium.*
3. Summary as a lineup poster with the rarest chip largest. *Small.*

## Arena and Game Zone — E 6 · A 6
**Problems.** Scores jump, deltas appear with no order, the end of the match is a modal with two numbers. Characters are silent. The board is flat.
**Changes.**
1. End-of-match tally ceremony: sockets add to the totals in sequence with ticks, then colour bonuses, then the winner. *Medium.*
2. Character quips on placement as small speech bubbles, in the silent-film register. *Medium.*
3. Sound for pick, flight, land, hits and ticks from the same kit. *Small.*
4. Result as a poster card: winner, both lineups, points earned. *Small.*

## cZone — E 6 · A 7
**Problems.** Badges sit on a gradient with no reaction; the visit view is a grid.
**Changes.**
1. Tap a placed chip for a small reaction (bounce and quip). *Small.*
2. Showcase: the front page features the chip you place first. *Small.*

## Onboarding — E 5 · A 4
**Problems.** A text modal welcomes the player. The first minutes are reading.
**Changes.**
1. First launch rips a starter pack. The pack teaches. *Medium.*

## Global
1. Sound kit module replacing beeps, with paired visual pulses. *Medium.*
2. Copy pass to title-card voice across the app. *Medium.*
3. Remove the top colour tab row; one blue only. *Small.*
4. Section tabs 12 px Michroma; body never Michroma. *Small.*

## Cut list
- Top colour tabs (COLLECT / COMPETE / ORBIT / cZONE).
- "What's on" ticker strip.
- Get Started strip and Orbit Centers on Home.
- Every sentence that explains a streak, a bonus or a pack.
- The generic "Sweet!" and "Let's go" buttons.
